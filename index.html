Service Details

Specialized Division

Electronic Security & Surveillance Architecture

Comprehensive electronic surveillance and life safety systems engineered for high-availability commercial and industrial assets.

Analogue and High Definition (HD) CCTV systems represent a cost-effective, proven surveillance option for commercial facilities. Designed for reliable perimeter coverage and straightforward deployment with stable coaxial architecture.
IP security systems represent the modern enterprise standard. IP architecture delivers multi-megapixel clarity, scalable network routing, encrypted remote management, and seamless integration with corporate low-current infrastructure.
Modern surveillance arrays with artificial intelligence built directly into cameras and recording units. Advanced algorithms perform automated object detection, person tracking, vehicle license categorization, and real-time perimeter alert triage.
