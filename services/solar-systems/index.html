Photovoltaic Engineering

Turnkey Solar Panel (PV) Solutions

From engineering design and structural calculations to installation and grid synchronisation, we execute scalable solar power generation for industrial, logistics, and infrastructure clients.

Rooftop Solar PV Systems

Turn your factory, warehouse, or commercial building rooftop into an active energy asset. We provide non-penetrating mounting systems, anti-dust module coating, and string/central inverters that reduce peak demand costs by up to 60%.

Ground-Mounted Arrays & Microgrids

Custom-engineered ground installations for remote industrial facilities, labor accommodation camps, and pump stations. Includes soil civil preparation, galvanized steel pilings, and dual-axis solar trackers for maximized irradiance capture.

Inverters & Battery Storage (BESS)

Smart hybrid inverters paired with high-cycle Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) storage banks. Enables seamless uninterrupted power transfer during grid fluctuations and provides steady, clean power 24/7 without diesel generators.

Autonomous Site Security

Solar-Powered CCTV & Mobile Surveillance Poles

Complete off-grid surveillance solutions that operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without electrical cabling, civil trenching, or wired internet connection.

Each solar CCTV pole or skid unit features a dedicated industrial-grade solar panel matched with an intelligent Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) charge controller and a high-capacity deep-cycle Lithium (LiFePO4) battery pack. This provides 5 to 7 days of continuous autonomy during heavily overcast skies, haze, or sandstorm conditions with zero downtime.
Equipped with high-gain multi-band 4G LTE and 5G cellular modems, our solar surveillance systems stream ultra-low-latency 4K and 1080p live video directly to your central control room, mobile phones, or Cloud VMS. High-speed local SD/NVR storage ensures full evidence retention even during temporary telecom blackouts.
Integrated deep-learning neural chips distinguish humans and vehicles with over 98% accuracy, eliminating nuisance alarms triggered by blowing sand or desert wildlife. When a restricted boundary is crossed, the camera triggers red/blue strobe lights, an audible warning siren, and dispatches real-time video clips to security officers.
